Friedrice

Fried rice is a popular Asian dish made from cooked rice that is stir-fried in a wok or frying pan, typically mixed with ingredients such as eggs, vegetables, seafood, or meat. It is often seasoned with soy sauce, garlic, and scallions, and is celebrated globally as a flavorful, versatile comfort food that is excellent for utilizing leftover ingredients.

ingredients

  • Rice
  • Egg
  • Aromatics
  • Vegetables
  • MSG
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Cook minced garlic and onions in hot oil until fragrant
  2. Push aromatics aside, pour in beaten eggs, and scramble
  3. Add frozen veggies and cold rice, mixing everything on high heat
  4. Stir in soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, adn pepperw
  5. Serve
