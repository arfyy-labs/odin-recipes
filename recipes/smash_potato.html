Smashed Potatoes

Smashed potatoes are a delicious side dish featuring small potatoes that are boiled until tender, gently flattened, and then roasted until golden and crispy. Celebrated globally as a comforting, textured treat, they feature a perfect contrast between a super crispy exterior and a fluffy, buttery interior.

ingredients

  • Small or baby potatoes
  • Olive oil or melted butter
  • Garlic powder
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Boil potatoes in salted water until fork-tender
  2. Drain potatoes and arrange them on a baking sheet
  3. Gently smash each potato flat using a glass or a fork
  4. Drizzle generously with oil or butter and season well
  5. Roast at high heat until crispy and golden brown
